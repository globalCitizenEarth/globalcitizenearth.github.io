Adventure Quote

It was too late to take risks now. I asked Tenzing to belay me strongly, and I started cutting a cautious line of steps up the ridge. Peering from side to side and thrusting with my ice axe, I tried to discover a possible cornice, but everything seemed solid and fire. I waved Tenzing up to me. A few more whacks of the ice-ax, a few very weary steps, and we were on the summit of Everist.
~Sir Edmund Hilary
