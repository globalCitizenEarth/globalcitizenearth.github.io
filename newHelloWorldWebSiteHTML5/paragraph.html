Machu Picchu

Located in the Peruvian Andes, the ancient Inca city of Machu Picchu was constructed in the 1400s at the height of the Inca Empire.

It was abandoned less than 100 years later when the Inca empire collased under Spanish conquest. Today, Machu Picchu is the most visited tourist attraction in Peru
